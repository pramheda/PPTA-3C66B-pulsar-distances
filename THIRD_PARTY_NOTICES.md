`sources/tremblay2026_v2_excerpts.tex` contains verbatim excerpts from the arXiv v2 source of **Jacob Cardinal Tremblay, Boris Goncharov, Rutger van Haasteren and co-authors (2026)**, *A Multimessenger Search for the Supermassive Black Hole Binary in 3C 66B with the Parkes Pulsar Timing Array*. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/ae3c98); [arXiv:2508.20007v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20007v2). The arXiv record carries the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence. The excerpts are unmodified; each block records its original line range and the full-file SHA-256.

`data/cited_measurements.json` records individual published measurements, with their locations, from Ding et al. (2023), Verbiest et al. (2009) and Jennings et al. (2018). `data/sky_positions.json` records the 3C 66B position printed in the paper and two pulsar positions from the public PPTA DR3 timing models. No third-party tables or files are redistributed.

The code in `scripts/`, the files in `results/` and the documentation are this independent audit. They do not imply endorsement by the authors.
